## What this changes in the design

1. **D2 is settled against us**: the tier's unit is *a block across all KV-cache groups, atomically*.
   Group-selective residency is expressible in the key but not loadable through `CPUOffloadingManager`,
   so "offload attention only, keep GDN state in HBM" is off the table unless we ship our own manager.
2. **The novelty list shrinks to what the evidence actually supports**: role/ETA/lease-driven ranking
   (proven to diverge from LRU on identical traffic), plus the negative results (hybrid granularity,
   tier↔allocator conflict, group atomicity), plus deployed-systems correctness gates. Pinning,
   reuse-count admission, and positional prefix identity are all **library features**.
3. **D1's answer to the original objection** is now first-party, not simulated: positional
   `(block_hash, group_idx)` keys mean a rolled-back tail costs only its own blocks. A single
   per-session hash (the 70 K-block proposal) would have failed this test — the concern was right
   about that design, and the engine already uses the better one.
